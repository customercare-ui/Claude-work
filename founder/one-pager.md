# Turbolux Home Finish: one page

**Verdict: Not yet**, computed from the numbers (`business-plan.md`). The unit makes money. Year 1 doesn't yet, and simulated buyers fall short of the bar. All amounts are ₹.

**The problem.** A Kochi family repainting today buys paint from a dealer and hires a contractor separately. Nobody owns the result. The top complaint about the big painting services is crews that stop turning up halfway (9 public reviews in `competitors.md`), followed by poor finish and bills that grow.

**The customer.** Kochi homeowners with a **deadline** (a wedding or function) or a **distance** (the owner works in the Gulf and the parents live in the house). They pay for certainty, not the lowest rate per sq ft.

**The offer and price.** A Turbolux-certified crew repaints the home with Turbolux paint:
- **₹89,000** for a 2BHK independent house, inside and out (test ₹95,000);
- **₹59,000** for a flat, inside only.

Prices include GST. The buyer gets:
- a written final price with a per-sq-ft breakdown;
- a signed finish date, with ₹2,000 off for every day late;
- 30% to book, 70% after the owner checks the work;
- one named coordinator with daily WhatsApp photos;
- a 3-year guarantee against bubbling and peeling.

**With three changes together** (price ₹95,000, crew rate −10%, paint at its real cost if it is 40% of retail), the CFO's tool shows year 1 at **+₹3,25,251** and cash needed of **₹11,99,977**.

**The evidence.**
- **Buyer panel (simulated):** 5% bought the first pitch and 20% the re-built offer. Trust objections halved, from 10 to 5. The remaining no's are the family painter, no local proof, and damp not covered.
- **Competitor gap:** no Kochi business publishes a fixed 2BHK price with a finish date backed by a penalty. Asian Paints could copy it within a season.
- **Real test:** not yet run. The founding-homes pre-sale, 23 Nov – 13 Dec 2026, is the first real evidence.

**What the money is for.**
- **Stage 1, now:** ≈ ₹60,000 to sell 10 founding homes at the real price with refundable 30% deposits.
- **Stage 2, only if ≥ 6 paid bookings come from ≥ 40 site visits:** about ₹18.4 lakh to cover the ₹8 lakh startup and the year 1 operating loss.
  - Startup: crew training and certification, site kits and moisture meters, booking page and CRM, launch marketing, legal review of the guarantee, and the founding-home discounts.
  - Running cost: a service team of 4 plus local ads, ₹2.04 lakh a month.

**The ask.**
1. Approve the **₹60,000 founding-homes test** to start Mon 23 Nov.
2. By Fri 20 Nov, decide **painter status** (with the accountant) and Turbolux's **paint transfer price**.
3. On **Mon 14 Dec**, decide go or no-go on stage 2 against the written success line.

*The buyer panel is simulated and the numbers are projections built mostly on estimates. Not financial, legal or tax advice.*
