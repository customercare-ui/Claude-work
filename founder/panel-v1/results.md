## What would flip a no

- A written penalty for every day they run late, taken off the bill, and the balance paid only after the work is finished and I have checked it, with the per-square-foot breakdown shown so I can compare.
- A site visit that gives a per-square-foot quote for my actual house, with damp-proofing of the outside walls included, priced below what the big-brand contractors quote me and backed by a written guarantee of several years against damp and peeling.
- A written guarantee of at least 3 years that covers labour and paint for bubbling, peeling and dampness patches. With that, if the site visit gives me a square-foot breakdown that comes in under my other two quotes, I'd book.
- A written guarantee that gives the number of years and says exactly what they will redo, plus two or three finished Turbolux homes in Kochi that I can see or whose owners I can call.
- A written finish date with a penalty or money back for every day they run late, and a finished home nearby I can go and look at.
- A written promise that ₹78,000 is the final bill whatever the site visit finds, plus a 2BHK they painted in Kochi that I can go and see myself, a few monsoons old and still not peeling.
- A written guarantee with a clear term, say five years, that covers redoing the affected walls at no cost, plus a price that is not much above what our own painter quotes.
- If our painter were not available this season, or if he himself told me Turbolux paint is better and he would use it, I would go with his suggestion.
- An interior-only flat price, with one named Turbolux person I can call who answers, and a crew that works within our building's contractor hours, offered when damp patches actually show up.
- A written guarantee of at least two to three years that covers bubbling and peeling even where dampness is the cause, with damp treatment of that wall included in the fixed price, and a promise to come back and redo it free.
- A per-square-foot quote from the site visit that comes in around ₹60,000, with the finish date and the peeling guarantee in writing, so my parents don't have to deal with the crew while I'm in Dubai.
- A smaller package for just the living room and the front exterior at around ₹25,000-30,000, finished on a fixed date before the wedding, that I can book and pay for online from abroad with photo updates sent to me.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
