The best time to paint a house in Kerala is from December to March, when the northeast monsoon has ended, humidity is lower and the walls are driest. Late October and November can also work for interiors and for exteriors on dry days, but afternoon thunderstorms are common. Avoid exterior painting from June to September, during the southwest monsoon, and be careful in April and May when pre-monsoon showers begin. If you plan to paint before a wedding, housewarming or festival, book your painter now, because December to March is also the busiest season.

Why does timing matter so much in Kerala?

Kerala gets two monsoons: the southwest monsoon from June to September, and the northeast monsoon (thulavarsham) from October to December. Together they bring close to 3,000 mm of rain a year, according to the India Meteorological Department, and humidity often stays above 80 percent.

Paint needs two things to perform: a dry wall and dry air while it cures. When walls are damp, the paint cannot bond properly, and that leads to peeling and flaking, bubbling and patchy finishes. When rain hits exterior paint before it has cured, it can wash off or leave streaks. Picking the right season is the cheapest way to make your paint last longer.

Can you paint a house during the monsoon?

You can paint interiors during the monsoon if the walls are dry and there is no active leak, but you should not paint exteriors while it is raining regularly. The main risks are:

  • Rain wash-off. Exterior emulsion usually needs several dry hours after application to resist rain. A sudden shower can streak or wash away fresh paint.
  • Trapped moisture. Damp plaster pushes moisture out through the new paint, causing blisters and peeling within weeks.
  • Slow drying. High humidity slows curing, so coats stay soft and pick up dust, and recoat times get longer.
  • Algae and fungus. Paint applied on a damp wall is more likely to develop green or black patches. See our guide on stopping algae and fungus on exterior walls.

If you must paint in the rainy season, use the monsoon to do the prep work instead: fix leaks, repair cracks and plan colours, then paint once the weather turns.

Should you paint the interior and exterior at different times?

Yes, splitting the job can save time and money. Interiors are protected from rain, so they can be painted in October and November while you wait for the exterior walls to dry out. The exterior can then follow in December to February. This also spreads your spending and makes it easier to get a good painter during peak season.

One rule applies either way: fix any exterior water seepage first. Interior paint on a wall that is letting in rain will fail no matter how well it is applied. Our guide on stopping wall dampness and seepage explains what to check.

How do you know your walls are dry enough to paint?

  1. Wait after the rain. Give exterior walls at least 2 to 3 dry, sunny days after heavy rain, and longer after a long wet spell.
  2. Use the plastic sheet test. Tape a 30 cm square of clear plastic to the wall for 24 hours. Droplets underneath mean the wall is still damp.
  3. Ask for a moisture meter reading. Many painters and dealers carry one. A low reading gives you confidence before priming.
  4. Check new plaster age. New cement plaster needs at least 28 days to cure, and more in humid weather. Read our guide on how to prepare new plaster for painting.
  5. Look for signs. Dark patches, a musty smell, white powdery salts or soft plaster all mean the wall needs more time or a damp treatment first.

How should you plan a paint job for the coming season?

With the southwest monsoon over, now is the right time to plan a dry-season paint job:

  • October: Inspect walls for damage from the rains. Note cracks, damp patches and algae. Our repainting guide helps you decide whether it is time for a full repaint.
  • November: Get quotes, fix leaks and choose colours. Paint interiors if you want to be ready for Christmas or a family function.
  • December to March: Paint the exterior. Start early in the day, avoid painting walls that are in strong direct sun, and stop by mid-afternoon if clouds build.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best season to paint a house in Kerala?

The dry season from December to March is best. Humidity is lowest, rain is rare, and walls have dried out after both monsoons.

Is it OK to paint the exterior of a house in October?

It is possible on dry days, but the northeast monsoon brings frequent afternoon showers in October and November. Paint early in the morning and check the forecast, or wait until December for the safest results.

How long after rain can I paint exterior walls?

Wait for at least 2 to 3 dry, sunny days after heavy rain, and check that the wall surface is fully dry before priming.

Can I paint interior walls during the monsoon?

Yes, if the walls are dry, there is no leak, and the rooms are well ventilated. Drying will take longer, so allow extra time between coats.

Is summer a good time to paint in Kerala?

March and early April are good. By May, heat and pre-monsoon showers make exterior painting harder. Avoid painting walls in strong midday sun, as the paint can dry too fast and bubble.
