A typical 2BHK house in Kerala (about 800 to 1,000 sq ft carpet area) needs roughly 40 to 55 litres of interior emulsion for two coats, 20 to 28 litres of interior primer, and 150 to 200 kg of wall putty if the walls are new. Painting the exterior as well adds about 30 to 45 litres of exterior emulsion and 15 to 22 litres of exterior primer. The exact figure depends on your paintable wall area, the number of coats and the paint's spreading rate, and below is the simple formula you can use to work it out for your own home in five minutes.

How do you calculate paint quantity for a 2BHK?

Every paint estimate comes down to three numbers: the area you are painting, how many coats you apply, and how many square feet one litre covers. The formula is:

Litres needed = (Paintable area in sq ft x Number of coats) / Coverage per litre per coat

Here is how to get each number:

  1. Paintable area. Measure each room's perimeter (add up the length of all four walls) and multiply by the wall height. A standard Kerala home has a 10 ft ceiling height. Subtract doors (about 21 sq ft each) and windows (about 15 sq ft each). Add the ceiling area (length x width) if you are painting ceilings too.
  2. Quick shortcut. If you do not want to measure every wall, multiply your carpet area by 3 to 3.5. This gives a reliable estimate of total interior wall plus ceiling area. For an 850 sq ft 2BHK, that is roughly 2,550 to 3,000 sq ft.
  3. Coats. Repainting in a similar shade usually needs 2 coats. A new house or a big colour change (dark to light) may need 3.
  4. Coverage. Most interior emulsions cover about 110 to 140 sq ft per litre per coat on a smooth, primed wall. Always check the pack or the product data sheet, because this number varies between products.

How much exterior paint does a 2BHK need?

Exterior area is measured differently. For an independent 2BHK house, measure the perimeter of the building and multiply by its height, then subtract large openings. A single-storey 2BHK house of around 1,000 sq ft built-up area typically has 1,400 to 1,900 sq ft of exterior wall, including parapets and sunshades.

Exterior emulsions usually cover a little less than interior paint because the surface is rougher, often 80 to 110 sq ft per litre per coat. With two coats, that works out to about 30 to 45 litres of exterior emulsion.

How much primer and putty will you need?

Primer and putty are where most homeowners underestimate. They are only needed in full quantity on new plaster or badly damaged walls. On a sound wall being repainted, you may only need putty for patch repairs and one coat of primer.

What changes the amount of paint you need?

  • Wall condition. Rough, porous or unprimed plaster can soak up 20 to 30 percent more paint.
  • Colour change. Going from a dark shade to a light one often needs an extra coat.
  • Ceiling height. A 10.5 or 11 ft ceiling adds 5 to 10 percent more wall area than the standard 10 ft.
  • Application method. Rollers usually waste less than brushes on large walls. Spray application can waste more.
  • Built-in wardrobes and tiles. Kitchen and bathroom walls that are tiled up to the ceiling can be subtracted completely.

How can you avoid buying too much or too little?

  1. Add 10 percent for wastage and touch-ups. Keep one sealed litre of each shade for future repairs.
  2. Buy in larger packs. A 20 L pack is usually cheaper per litre than four 4 L packs, and a single batch keeps the shade consistent.
  3. Buy one room at a time if you are unsure. Paint the largest room first, note how much it used, and scale up.
  4. Ask your painter for a written quantity estimate. A good painter will list putty, primer and emulsion separately. Our guide on how much it costs to paint a house in Kerala explains how to check the numbers.

Frequently asked questions

How many litres of paint are needed for a 2BHK flat?

For two coats on interior walls and ceilings, a 2BHK flat of 800 to 1,000 sq ft carpet area typically needs 40 to 55 litres of emulsion. Add 20 to 28 litres of primer and, for new walls, 150 to 200 kg of putty.

How many square feet does 1 litre of paint cover?

Most interior emulsions cover 110 to 140 sq ft per litre per coat on a smooth, primed wall. Exterior emulsions usually cover 80 to 110 sq ft per litre per coat. Check the product label for the exact figure.

How many coats of paint does a 2BHK need?

Two coats of emulsion over one coat of primer is the standard for repainting. New plaster or a major colour change may need a third coat of emulsion.

What is the quick formula for paint area from carpet area?

Multiply your carpet area by 3 to 3.5 to estimate total interior wall and ceiling area. For an 850 sq ft flat, that is about 2,550 to 3,000 sq ft of paintable surface.

Is it better to buy extra paint?

Yes. Buy about 10 percent extra. Shades can vary slightly between batches, so having leftover paint from the same batch makes future touch-ups invisible.
